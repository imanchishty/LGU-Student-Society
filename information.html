Upcoming Event: Spring Tech & Design Expo

Join us this April at the main LGU campus for our flagship Spring Tech & Design Expo. This event brings together computer science and design students to showcase their semester projects, ranging from web applications to glassmorphic UI prototypes.

Attendees will have the opportunity to network with alumni, attend mini-workshops on modern web technologies, and participate in a live UI/UX design challenge. Don't miss this chance to expand your portfolio and meet fellow creators!
